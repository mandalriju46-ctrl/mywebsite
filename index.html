About Us

Fresh & Vibrant

Tropico is Mowa's go-to spot for fresh tropical drinks, loaded bites, and good vibes. Tucked inside Horizon Tower at Dubey Colony, we're your neighbourhood escape for something delicious.

Everything on our menu is 100% vegetarian — from thick cold coffees and tropical juices to cheesy pizzas and crunchy wraps. Made fresh, served fast, always satisfying.
